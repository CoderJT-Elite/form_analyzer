How it coaches

Camera in, coaching out.

Three steps happen on every single frame, entirely on your phone.

01 — WATCH

Camera becomes skeleton

Google's on-device pose model turns the camera feed into joint positions in real time. The image itself is discarded immediately — only the skeleton survives, in memory, for a fraction of a second.

02 — JUDGE

Every rep gets checked

Depth, back angle, tempo, and left-right symmetry are measured against real thresholds for the exercise you picked, not a generic "good form" guess.

03 — SPEAK

One cue, not a flood

The single most important issue gets said out loud. You never hear five corrections stacked on top of each other mid-rep.

What "most important" means

Cues are ranked. A higher tier always cuts off a lower one — so a safety warning is the only thing you'll hear until it's resolved.

Safety
Rounded back, unsafe joint angle
Setup
Wrong orientation to the camera
Depth & range
Came up short of full range
Tempo
Dropping instead of lowering
Encouragement
Clean rep, said out loud
